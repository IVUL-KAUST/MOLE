# Guidelines for MESD Schema
In this task we want to extract metadata from papers. Here is a discribtion of the mdatadata:

1. **Title**: the title of the paper
2. **Paper_Link**: the direct link to access the paper
3. **Link**: all the links in the paper
4. **Author**: the authors of the paper
5. **Authoraffiliation**: the authors' affiliations of the paper
6. **Doi**: the doi of the paper
7. **Email**: the email of the authors
8. **Date**: all the dates in the paper
9. **Abstract**: the abstract of the paper
