# Guidelines for MESD Schema
In this task we want to extract metadata from papers. Here is a discribtion of the mdatadata:

1. **Paper_Link**: the direct link to access the paper
2. **Title**: the title of the paper
3. **Authors**: the authors of the paper
4. **Abstract**: the abstract of the paper
5. **Year**: the year of the paper
6. **Field**: the fields of study of the paper
